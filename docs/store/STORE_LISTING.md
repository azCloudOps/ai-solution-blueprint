## Full description (both stores, under 4,000 characters)

Turn an AI idea into a production-ready design in one guided session.

AI Solution Blueprint helps architects, platform engineers, IT leaders and makers make the decisions that decide whether an AI project reaches production, in the order they need to be made.

EIGHT GUIDED STEPS
1. Idea: owner, problem, success metric and value hypothesis
2. Use case: a recommended solution pattern with the reasons and the alternatives
3. Data: sensitivity, freshness, volume and residency
4. Actions and risk: a risk tier and the governance controls that apply
5. Platform: a reference architecture on Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud or a portable multi-cloud stack
6. Scale and cost: monthly run cost, cost per task and spending guardrails
7. Readiness: a stage-gated checklist from idea to production
8. Blueprint: the complete design, ready to share

WHAT YOU GET
- Six solution patterns: prompted model, retrieval augmented generation (RAG), tool-using agent, multi-agent system, fine-tuned model and predictive machine learning
- Governance controls mapped to the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001
- A generated architecture diagram with every cloud service flagged for verification
- An evaluation plan with metrics for each pattern
- A delivery roadmap with indicative timelines
- Share any blueprint as Markdown, JSON or an SVG diagram through the share sheet
- Keep as many blueprints as you like on your device

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything runs on your device. No account, no tracking, no analytics and no network requests. Your designs never leave your phone or tablet unless you share them.

UPDATED EVERY MONTH
Cloud AI services change constantly. A new version with a reviewed knowledge base ships every month.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
AI Solution Blueprint is built by azCloudOps and released under the MIT License. The same tool runs on the web and from the command line.

The risk tier is a design aid, not legal advice. Cost estimates use example rates until you enter your own.
