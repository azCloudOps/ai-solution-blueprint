Privacy

AI Solution Blueprint privacy policy

AI Solution Blueprint does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

This policy covers the AI Solution Blueprint apps on Google Play and the App Store, the web app hosted on GitHub Pages, and the command line tool. All are published by Manoj Annavajjala under the azCloudOps name.

What the app does with your information

  • The answers you enter and the blueprints you create are saved only in the app's storage on your device.
  • The app makes no network requests. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • We never see your blueprints. We cannot recover them for you.

When information leaves your device

Only when you choose to share it. If you share a Markdown, JSON or SVG file through your device's share sheet, or send a share link, it goes to the app or person you pick and is handled under their privacy terms. Share links carry the blueprint's answers inside the link itself, so anyone with the link can read them. Do not put confidential details in a blueprint you share by link.

Deleting your data

Delete a blueprint in My blueprints, or uninstall the app to remove everything. In a browser, clearing site data for the web app removes your saved blueprints.

App stores and websites

Google, Apple and GitHub may collect data when you download or open the app through their services, under their own privacy policies. This website, azcloudops.org, has its own privacy policy for visits to the site.

Children

The app is designed for IT professionals and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: use the contact form on azcloudops.org or open an issue at github.com/azcloudops/ai-solution-blueprint.
